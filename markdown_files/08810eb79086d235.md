# TechNest Electronics — Customer Support Knowledge Base

*Internal reference document for the AI support agent. This document is the single source of truth the assistant is allowed to answer from.*

---

## 1. Company Overview

TechNest Electronics is an online retailer selling smartphones, laptops, audio devices, and smart-home accessories, shipping across Pakistan with select international shipping to the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and the UK. TechNest was founded in 2021 and operates two fulfillment centers: one in Lahore and one in Karachi. Customer support is available Monday through Saturday, 9 AM to 9 PM PKT, through live chat, email (support@technest.example), and WhatsApp.

## 2. Refund Policy

### 2.1 Standard Return Window
Customers may request a refund or return within **7 days** of the delivery date. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, with all accessories, manuals, and the original invoice included. Items showing signs of use, missing accessories, or damaged packaging will not qualify for a full refund and may only be eligible for a partial refund at TechNest's discretion.

### 2.2 Non-Returnable Items
The following categories are **not eligible** for return or refund except in cases of manufacturing defect:
- Earphones, earbuds, and other items that come into direct contact with the ear or skin, once the seal is broken
- Gift cards and store credit vouchers
- Software licenses and digital downloads
- Items marked "Clearance" or "Final Sale" at the time of purchase
- Custom or made-to-order configurations (e.g., laptops with custom RAM/SSD upgrades)

### 2.3 Refund Process
1. Customer initiates a return request through the "My Orders" page or by contacting support with the order number.
2. Support verifies eligibility (within the 7-day window, item condition, category).
3. If eligible, a return shipping label is issued at no cost if the return is due to a TechNest error (wrong item shipped, defective unit, damaged in transit). If the return is due to a change of mind, the customer bears the return shipping cost.
4. Once the returned item is received and inspected at the fulfillment center (typically 2–4 business days), the refund is processed to the original payment method.
5. Refunds to credit/debit cards take 5–10 business days to reflect, depending on the issuing bank. Refunds to TechNest Wallet are instant. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded via bank transfer, which requires the customer's IBAN and takes 3–5 business days after the IBAN is provided.

### 2.4 Partial Refunds
Partial refunds (a percentage of the item price, with the remainder withheld as a restocking or damage fee) apply when:
- The item is missing original accessories or packaging (10–20% deduction)
- The item shows minor cosmetic wear inconsistent with "unused" condition (up to 30% deduction)
- The return is initiated after the 7-day window but within 14 days, at TechNest's discretion (store credit only, no cash refund)

### 2.5 Damaged or Defective Items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, the customer must report it within **48 hours of delivery** with photos or a short video showing the issue. TechNest covers full return shipping in this case, and the customer may choose between:
- A free replacement of the same item (subject to stock)
- A full refund
- Store credit with a 5% bonus value

## 3. Shipping Policy

### 3.1 Delivery Timelines
- **Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad (major cities):** 1–2 business days
- **Other cities in Pakistan:** 3–5 business days
- **International (UAE, Saudi Arabia, UK):** 7–14 business days, subject to customs clearance

### 3.2 Shipping Charges
Standard shipping within Pakistan is free for orders above PKR 5,000. Orders below this threshold incur a flat PKR 250 shipping fee. Express shipping (next-day delivery in major cities) is available for an additional PKR 500. International shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination and are non-refundable once the order has shipped, even if the order is later cancelled or returned.

### 3.3 Order Tracking
Once an order is dispatched, the customer receives a tracking link via SMS and email. Order status moves through the following stages: **Order Placed → Processing → Packed → Shipped → Out for Delivery → Delivered**. If an order shows "Shipped" for more than 3 business days within the same city without movement, the customer should contact support so a trace can be filed with the courier.

### 3.4 Failed Delivery Attempts
Couriers attempt delivery up to **3 times**. If all three attempts fail (customer unavailable, incorrect address, refused delivery), the order is returned to the TechNest warehouse and the customer is notified. The customer can then request re-shipment (additional shipping fee applies) or a refund (minus original shipping cost, if shipping was not free).

## 4. Warranty Policy

### 4.1 Standard Warranty Coverage
All new electronics sold by TechNest come with a **1-year manufacturer warranty** against manufacturing defects, unless otherwise stated on the product page. This covers hardware failures that occur under normal use. It does **not** cover:
- Physical damage (drops, cracked screens, liquid damage)
- Damage from unauthorized repairs or third-party modifications
- Normal wear and tear (battery degradation after 12+ months is considered normal wear)
- Software issues caused by unofficial firmware or rooting/jailbreaking

### 4.2 Extended Warranty
Customers can purchase TechNest Care, an extended warranty add-on, at checkout for select high-value items (laptops, flagship smartphones). TechNest Care extends coverage to 2 years total and additionally covers one incident of accidental damage (cracked screen or liquid spill) per year, subject to a service fee of PKR 2,000–5,000 depending on the device category.

### 4.3 Warranty Claim Process
1. Customer contacts support with the order number and a description of the issue.
2. Support may ask for troubleshooting steps to rule out software issues before approving a hardware claim.
3. If approved, the customer ships the item to the nearest TechNest service center (shipping cost reimbursed if the claim is approved).
4. Diagnosis takes 3–7 business days. If confirmed as a covered defect, the item is repaired or replaced (TechNest's discretion) at no cost.
5. If the issue is found to be outside warranty coverage (e.g., physical damage), the customer is notified with a repair quote before any chargeable work is done.

## 5. Order Management

### 5.1 Order Cancellation
Orders can be cancelled free of charge as long as the status is still "Order Placed" or "Processing." Once an order status changes to "Packed" or later, it cannot be cancelled — the customer must wait for delivery and then initiate a return under the refund policy above.

### 5.2 Changing Delivery Address
A delivery address can be changed only while the order is in "Order Placed" or "Processing" status, and only after identity verification (the customer must confirm the name and phone number on the account). Once an order is "Shipped," the address cannot be changed; the customer may refuse delivery and place a new order instead.

### 5.3 Order Status Definitions
- **Order Placed:** Payment confirmed, order queued for processing.
- **Processing:** Warehouse is picking and packing the item(s).
- **Packed:** Item is boxed and awaiting courier pickup.
- **Shipped:** Courier has collected the package.
- **Out for Delivery:** Package is with the local delivery rider for same-day delivery.
- **Delivered:** Package has been handed to the customer or an authorized recipient.
- **Cancelled:** Order was cancelled before shipping.
- **Returned:** Item was sent back to TechNest after delivery.

## 6. Payment Methods

TechNest accepts the following payment methods:
- Cash on Delivery (COD) — available for orders under PKR 100,000
- Debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard) via a secure payment gateway
- Bank transfer (direct transfer with order confirmation upon proof of payment)
- TechNest Wallet (store credit balance, top-up available, non-withdrawable but usable on any purchase)
- EasyPaisa and JazzCash mobile wallets

COD orders above PKR 50,000 require a 20% advance payment via card or bank transfer to confirm the order, refundable if the order is cancelled before shipping.

## 7. Product Support & Troubleshooting

### 7.1 Common Smartphone Issues
- **Battery drains fast:** Usually caused by background apps or an outdated OS. Support first asks the customer to check battery usage stats in Settings and update to the latest software version before escalating to a warranty claim.
- **Phone won't charge:** Support asks the customer to try a different cable and charger first, since cable failure is the most common cause and is not covered under warranty unless the cable itself was defective out of the box.
- **Screen flickering:** Treated as a potential hardware defect and is eligible for a warranty claim if it began within the coverage period and there's no physical damage.

### 7.2 Laptop Support
Laptops purchased through TechNest come pre-installed with the operating system selected at checkout (Windows 11 Home/Pro or no OS for Linux users). TechNest does not provide support for third-party software installed after delivery. Hardware issues (keyboard, trackpad, display, battery) that arise within the warranty period are covered under section 4 above.

### 7.3 Smart Home Devices
Smart plugs, cameras, and speakers sold by TechNest require the TechNest Home app (available on iOS and Android) for setup. Devices connect over 2.4GHz Wi-Fi only; 5GHz-only networks are not supported and are the most common cause of failed setup. If a device fails to connect after following the in-app setup guide, support will walk the customer through a factory reset before considering a defect claim.

## 8. Escalation & Human Handoff

The AI assistant should escalate to a human support agent, rather than attempt to resolve the issue itself, in the following cases:
- The customer explicitly asks to speak to a human agent
- The request involves changing payment details, disputing a charge, or a fraud/security concern
- The request involves a legal complaint or threat of legal action
- The customer's issue does not match any documented policy or troubleshooting step above, and inventing an answer would risk giving incorrect information
- The customer is requesting a refund or address change outside the documented policy windows, which requires manual approval

When escalating, the assistant should inform the customer that their case has been forwarded to a human agent and give an expected response time of within 24 hours on business days.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I return an item if I just don't like it anymore, with no defect?**
A: Yes, within the 7-day return window, as long as it's unused and in original packaging. This falls under a standard "change of mind" return, and the customer covers return shipping.

**Q: How do I know if my order qualifies for free shipping?**
A: Any order with a subtotal of PKR 5,000 or more automatically qualifies for free standard shipping within Pakistan; this is applied automatically at checkout.

**Q: My order has been "Shipped" for 5 days with no movement. What should I do?**
A: Contact support with the order number so a trace can be filed with the courier, since anything beyond 3 business days without movement in the same city is considered delayed.

**Q: Does TechNest Care cover a cracked screen from a drop?**
A: Yes, but only if the customer purchased the TechNest Care extended warranty add-on at checkout, and only once per year, subject to the service fee outlined in section 4.2. The standard 1-year manufacturer warranty does not cover accidental physical damage.

**Q: I paid via bank transfer but my order still shows "Order Placed." Why?**
A: Bank transfer orders move to "Processing" only after TechNest confirms receipt of payment, which can take up to 1 business day. If it has been longer, the customer should contact support with proof of payment.
